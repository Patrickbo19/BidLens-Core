# Autonomous income opportunity review — 30 August 2026

The strongest new lane is CreateOS's monetized Skills marketplace, but revenue is **not guaranteed**. The advantage is structural: a single zero-dependency service can be sold as recurring metered calls and as a deployable template, with hosting, discovery, billing, fraud handling, and attribution kept on-platform.

## Ranked shortlist

| Rank | Lane | $0 startup | Owner labor after launch | Economics | Evidence and verdict |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CreateOS Skill + template | Yes on stated free tier | Near zero | 80% of Skill calls; 70% of template sales; no threshold | Newly documented 26 Jun 2026; 100,000+ builders and 150+ templates are platform-reported. Best new distribution/economics combination, so this package was built. |
| 2 | Poe priced prompt bot | Yes | Near zero | Creator-set per-response price | Existing BidLens Poe pack is already complete. Current creator features remain active, but the unresolved Poe account/publication gate prevents another asset from improving its near-term state. |
| 3 | Apify Actor Store | Yes; customers can bear compute | Low to moderate maintenance | 80% of Actor revenue minus usage costs, or 80% when costs pass through | Strong, proven marketplace structure. RFP/procurement analysis is now crowded and comparable actors show only low single-digit user counts, so a new clone does not beat this build. |
| 4 | Notion paid templates | Yes | Support is contractually expected | Seller keeps sale price less 8% + $0.40 | Good merchant-of-record economics, but discovery is saturated and required support weakens passivity. |
| 5 | Canva Creators | Yes | Low after acceptance | Usage-based royalty pool | Large audience and true royalties, but acceptance is required and payout per use is not published; slower and less controllable. |
| 6 | Amazon Merch on Demand | Yes | Low | Royalty per sale; fulfillment handled | Fully managed and legitimate, but account approval plus intense design competition make expected first revenue slower. |
| 7 | Adobe Stock generative content | Yes | Low | 33%-style stock royalty schedule; $0.33 minimum on common large subscriptions; $25 payout threshold | AI content is accepted when labeled and compliant, but unit payouts are too small for this queue. |
| 8 | Algora / IssueHunt code bounties | Yes | No ongoing labor, but substantial one-time work | Fixed bounty | Current public boards contain some live bounties, but many are stale, claimed, or low-value; none reviewed had a better risk-adjusted path than a reusable metered asset. |

## Economics without invented traffic

At the suggested 5-credit call price:

| Paid calls / month | Gross | Publisher at stated 80% |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 100 | $5 | $4 |
| 1,000 | $50 | $40 |
| 10,000 | $500 | $400 |

These are arithmetic scenarios, not forecasts. No official source found exposed marketplace-wide paid-call counts or independent seller payout distributions. The asset should therefore launch with $0 promotion spend and be judged on organic discovery data after 30 days.
